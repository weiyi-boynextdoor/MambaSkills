# Poster Image Reference

Use this reference when generating ecommerce poster images.

## Image Prompt Priorities

- The product must be the obvious visual hero.
- The scene must support the selling point.
- The composition must leave clean space for ad text.
- The output should look like a finished commercial poster, not a mood board.
- The product shape, color, material, packaging, logo placement, and brand cues should stay consistent with user input or reference images.

## Common Layouts

- Center hero: product centered, simple background, strong thumbnail readability.
- Diagonal hero: product angled with motion or ingredient cues, good for social ads.
- Split benefit: product on one side, clean text area on the other.
- Lifestyle scene: product in use, shallow depth, clear buying context.
- Bundle stack: multiple SKUs arranged by value, with offer space.
- Seasonal frame: product hero with restrained campaign props.

## Platform Notes

- Square: strong center product, readable at small size.
- Vertical story: tall composition, product in upper or middle third, text-safe area.
- Marketplace thumbnail: minimal props, high contrast, product details visible.
- Banner: horizontal negative space, product anchored left or right.

## Generation Prompt Checklist

Before generating, ensure the prompt includes:

- Product and category.
- Audience or scenario.
- Selling angle.
- Composition and camera angle.
- Lighting and background.
- Brand tone and color direction.
- Text-safe area.
- Aspect ratio or platform.

## Avoid

- Heavy text rendered inside the image unless the user explicitly asks for it.
- Fake logos or brand marks.
- Unreadable typography.
- Overcrowded prop styling.
- Unrealistic product deformation.
- Unsupported claims.

## Post-Generation Review

If the user asks for critique or revision, check:

- Product clarity.
- Visual hierarchy.
- Text-safe space.
- Platform fit.
- Brand consistency.
- Claim safety.
